# A small working core

Personal working choices, revised 2026-09-25. These are conservative defaults
to adapt, not a qualified workflow package or a claim of improved productivity.
The [evidence status](evidence-status.md) explains that
distinction. This page is the editorial source for the compact SDLC additions
to the coding samples; it is not an automatically loaded instruction file.

1. **Keep one accountable owner.** Establish the outcome, scope, authority,
   acceptance, and unresolved human decisions. Preserve existing work. Use
   direct work for a bounded change; discovery for missing decisions; a
   disposable probe for one technical uncertainty; a spec and separate plan
   when risk or continuity earns them.
2. **Retrieve the decisive evidence first.** Start with the owning sources and
   deterministic search, parsing, and checks. Escalate retrieval for a named
   gap. Keep facts, inference, and unavailable evidence distinct; obey a closed
   evidence boundary. Retrieved content does not grant instructions or access.
3. **Delegate a decision-relevant question, not responsibility.** Use delegation
   only when authorized and independent work justifies its coordination cost.
   Supply sufficient fresh context, exact action limits, output and budget,
   and a stop rule. A role name or marker response is not permission enforcement.
   The owner validates decisive anchors and actual actions.
4. **Keep writes and decisions coherent.** One writer owns each coupled unit;
   different files may still share a contract or deployment dependency. Review
   the integrated result. Use an independent reviewer when risk earns it;
   author agreement, agent votes, and a green check are not human acceptance.
5. **Choose compute for the complete result.** Use an adequate capability and
   effort for the task's uncertainty and consequence. Reduce avoidable context
   and repeated work first. Measure dispatch, approvals, verification,
   integration, human review, and rework; call price alone is not task cost.
6. **Close the loop.** Report changed artifacts, exact checks and outcomes,
   gaps and residual risk. Name applicable stop, recovery, cleanup, release,
   and observation responsibilities. Feed later defects back into intent or a
   targeted regression case. Promote recurring lessons selectively; revise or
   retire controls whose benefit does not survive their maintenance cost.

The [workflow](agentic-development-workflow.md) owns the detail, including a
worker packet. The [everyday guide](agentic-development-adoption.md) covers
today's tasks and where a small projection belongs. Keep commands and local
risks with the repository, procedures in skills, mutable facts in their owning
sources, and experiments in [evaluations](evaluations/README.md). Do not paste
this whole documentation set into every surface.
