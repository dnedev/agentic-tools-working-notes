# Usable agentic-development notes — revision intent

Status: documentation revision, 2026-09-24. Maintainer: this repository's author.
Archived with the [revision records](../README.md) to preserve its intent and
acceptance separately from everyday guidance.

## Intent

Make these personal notes usable for everyday work without reading the whole
research program. Preserve one engineer's n=1 evidence and failed treatments;
offer a small set of working principles, an on-demand lifecycle guide, and
explicit adaptations for Codex, Claude Code, and Cowork. They must be useful
without joining an internal program or installing an orchestration system.

The reader should be able to choose the lightest suitable task lane, find the
owning evidence, retain one accountable integrator, decide whether delegation
is worth trying, and hand over an inspectable result. Internal colleagues and
public readers get the same sanitized general material; internal context and
contribution attribution remain in their approved private location.

## Boundaries

- Root `AGENTS.md` governs contributors to this notes repository. User-level
  examples are optional personal guidance; surface notes explain mechanics;
  skills own conditional procedures; evaluation material owns experiments.
- Reading a principle is not experimental qualification. Conservative choices
  under uncertainty must be labeled separately from comparative findings.
- Exact live model choices, installation, permission enforcement, remote
  publication, and live infrastructure are outside this documentation change.
- Private inputs are read-only. No private transcript, source identifier,
  live configuration, or employer-specific design is copied into tracked text.
- Private source material stays in ignored `_input/`; generated drafts, reviews,
  and exports belong in ignored `_output/`. Sanitized research and completed
  revision records may move to a tracked archive while retaining their links
  and evidence limits. Archiving does not complete or retire pending research.
- The public checkout has a separate history. Prepare sharing guidance, never
  merge, mirror, or publish the private history.

## Completion and maintenance

This revision is complete when the artifacts meet the criteria above, not when
the underlying workflow has proved a productivity gain. Behavior probes,
representative comparisons, installation, and publication remain separate
actions. A failed adoption probe returns that surface to manual task guidance;
it does not weaken the evidence or permission contract to obtain a pass.

The [evidence status](../../harness/evidence-status.md) retains the decisions and the
[archived backlog](research-backlog.md) retains unfinished research. Maintain
surface mechanics in their owning notes. The existing
[selective-derivation decision](../../harness/decisions/0001-selective-skill-derivation.md)
still applies; this revision selects no new stack or deployment shape.
