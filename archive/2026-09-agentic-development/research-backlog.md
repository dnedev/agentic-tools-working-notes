## A1 — First use and artifact continuity

Use one already authorized real task and the existing host setup. Preserve its
intent, criteria, evidence, diff/output, verification, and review disposition.
For a task with no release authority, hand off a reviewed candidate and name
pending operational checks. For authorized operation, record its success/failure
signal, observation owner, recovery boundary, and subsequent defects.

Acceptance:

- a fresh reader can resume from artifacts without reconstructing the chat;
- the chosen lane fits uncertainty and risk, with no unnecessary spec/prototype;
- checks and independent review cover both supplied intent and reachable risk;
- actual actions remain inside scope; later failures can reopen a criterion;
- framing, approvals, review, rework, and unavailable telemetry are explicit;
- usability is not reported as a comparative productivity win.

## C3b — Useful fresh-packet comparison

Status: design gate, unexecuted and unscheduled. This replaces a mechanical
rerun of the original R1 audit. That fixture's saturated baseline, overlapping
questions, and unavailable active-time measure could not support its intended
cost win. Its historical failure does not answer the compliant-delegation question.

Before paying for model work, freeze:

1. one representative task with two disjoint read-heavy questions, an expected
   answer key owned independently of the runner, and a named result consumer;
2. current source revision, prompt, model, effort, tools, permissions, context,
   output contract, and cleanup; verify the host's actual spawn/wait controls;
3. a cheap role/context preflight on that host; observed marker compliance and
   enforced tool denial must remain distinct;
4. baseline: one owner answers both questions; treatment: the same owner
   answers one while one fresh-packet worker answers the other, then the owner
   validates and integrates;
5. repetitions, interleaved order, grading/blinding limits, quality floor,
   invalidation, and latency/resource/human-review trade-off before either arm;
6. measures the host can actually expose, including approval interruptions and
   end-to-end time; unavailable active time cannot be the decisive win metric;
7. stop for role/permission breach, budget exhaustion, or a predeclared futility
   condition. A corrected packet starts a new version.

Acceptance:

- every valid trial passes critical quality and action-boundary criteria;
- the complete result meets the predeclared observable comparison rule;
- failures, invalid attempts, retries, integration, and human review are retained;
- insufficient measurement yields `more evidence`, not promotion;
- a synthetic result advances only to a representative pilot, not auto-dispatch.

No fixed worker count beyond the first treatment, trial count, or token ceiling
is claimed to be optimal. Multi-worker and independent-writer treatments are
separate experiments. One writer per coupled ownership unit still applies.

## C4 — Model, then effort, after useful topology qualifies

Held behind C3b. Keep the retained task and topology fixed, compare capability
while holding effort as constant as the host supports, then compare effort
within a retained model. If effort cannot be matched, describe a configuration
comparison rather than pretending capability alone changed. Record effective
settings and fallbacks; changed automatic delegation behavior is a topology
change. Evaluate total quality and effort, not a model's call price.
