# Archive

Sanitized research and completed revision records that support the notes but
do not belong in the everyday reading path. These are dated snapshots, not
active task instructions. Archiving an open question does not answer it;
unfinished experiments remain unfinished and require a fresh decision to run.
The dated [evidence status](../harness/evidence-status.md) lives with the
guidance and governs where these records disagree with it. The author's broader
roadmap and research plan, which recorded a personal research program and
continuity, are kept privately; the evidence status and backlog carry the
results and open work.

For current use, start with [adoption](../harness/agentic-development-adoption.md)
or the [workflow](../harness/agentic-development-workflow.md). The
[spec-driven skill](../harness/skills/spec-driven-change/SKILL.md) and the
live [evaluation instruments](../harness/evaluations/README.md) remain with the
usable guidance. A fixture's availability is not qualification of its treatment.

Private sources belong in ignored `_input/`; private drafts, reviews, handoffs,
and exports belong in ignored `_output/`. Neither directory is part of this
archive or a public export. Review and sanitize an artifact before deliberately
promoting it into tracked documentation. Do not copy private originals here
merely to put them out of the way.
